## Full Document
🚨 BREAKING: Claude can now design logos that look like they cost $10,000.

I tested it myself.

The final result cost me exactly $0.

Here are the 7 prompts I used:

Save this for later 

---

1. Brand Foundation Prompt

“Imagine you're an elite brand designer. 

Develop a logo for [brand name] that captures [core value] and speaks directly to [audience]. 

The design should feel sophisticated, timeless, and instantly recognizable around the world. 

Stay away from short-lived trends and prioritize a look that will endure for years.” 

---

2. Luxury Minimalism Prompt

“Create a sleek and luxurious logo for [brand name] with a minimalist aesthetic.

Focus on clean shapes, precise spacing, and a refined color scheme featuring black, white, and subtle gold details. 

Aim for the same level of elegance and simplicity associated with iconic global brands.” 

---

3. Typography-First Prompt

“Design a typography-focused logo for [brand name] that relies on distinctive letterforms, precise kerning, and clever use of negative space. 

The wordmark should be strong enough to stand on its own and remain memorable even without an accompanying icon or symbol.” 

---

4. Symbol + Meaning Prompt

“Create a logo for [brand name] that features a distinctive symbol inspired by [idea/mission].

 The mark should be clean and instantly recognizable while carrying enough depth and meaning to reflect the brand’s story and purpose.” 

---

5. Scalability Prompt

“Design a versatile logo system for [brand name] that performs seamlessly across every platform—from favicons and app icons to website headers and merchandise. 

Ensure the design remains clear, recognizable, and visually striking at both small and large scales.” 

---

6. Competitive Edge Prompt

“Research the top brands in [industry], then create a logo for [brand name] that feels distinctive while maintaining trust and professionalism within the market.

Stay away from overused design trends and the common clichés adopted by competitors.” 

---

7. Presentation Prompt (The Secret)

“Showcase the logo as if it were part of a $500,000 branding campaign. 

Display it across premium mockups—including business cards, product packaging, websites, and billboards.

 Add a concise explanation that outlines the creative choices behind the design and how they support the brand’s positioning in the market.”
