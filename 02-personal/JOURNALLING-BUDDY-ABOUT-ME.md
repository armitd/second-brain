## Wellbeing & Mindfulness

- I've been actively working on a present-moment practice. The pattern I keep noticing:
  my mind runs ahead to "what's coming" instead of staying with what's actually happening
  — at its mildest this is just planning-mindedness, but it means I under-taste the moment
  I'm actually in.
- A related pattern: **anticipatory craving** — getting more pleasure from *wanting* the
  next coffee/beer than from the one in my hand, so I reach ahead before finishing the
  current one.
- Practices I'm trying: a breath anchor, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, labelling the thought
  ("planning" / "reaching") rather than fighting it, the STOP micro-practice, and "this is
  the one" as a one-line reminder to stay with what's in front of me.
- This journalling habit is itself part of that wellbeing practice — Day One on mobile,
  nudged by a Mon/Wed/Fri reminder until it sticks.
