# Thread: 7 Prompts for Logo Design with Claude

## Summary
A sequence of 7 prompt templates for producing professional-looking logo design concepts with Claude, moving from brand foundation through to a "presentation" prompt that showcases the result as if part of a $500k branding campaign.

## Key Points
1. Brand Foundation — establish core value and audience
2. Luxury Minimalism — clean shapes, restrained palette
3. Typography-First — wordmark relying on letterforms and negative space
4. Symbol + Meaning — an icon tied to brand mission
5. Scalability — versatile across favicon to billboard
6. Competitive Edge — differentiate from category norms
7. Presentation — mock up across premium collateral with rationale
